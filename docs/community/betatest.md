# Become a Beta Tester

We are looking for a few **beta testers** to help us test upcoming Kenolab labs and content before their public release.

As a beta tester, you'll help us identify bugs, balance difficulty, and provide feedback to improve the overall experience.

::: info What do you get?
This is a **voluntary role**, so there is no financial compensation.

However, beta testers will receive a **dedicated Discord role** to highlight their contribution to the project.

And if we ever meet IRL, **the beer is on us 🍺**.

Interested in helping us?

Get in touch with us through our [Support page](/introduction/support).
:::

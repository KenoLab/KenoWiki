# Hints & Writeups Policy

To keep Kenolab challenges fair and enjoyable for everyone, please follow these rules when sharing hints, solutions, or writeups.

## Hints

Hints and solutions **must not be shared in public channels**.

Sharing information that reveals part of a challenge or helps other players progress can spoil the experience for the community.

- Do not share hints, flags, credentials, commands, or solutions in public channels.
- Players are allowed to help each other privately through **Direct Messages (DMs)**.
- Repeatedly sharing hints publicly after warnings may result in a **temporary ban from the Kenolab platform**.

::: warning Be careful with spoilers
Even a small piece of information can reveal an important part of a challenge. If you are unsure whether something is considered a hint, share it privately instead.
:::

## Writeups

We encourage players to create and publish writeups about Kenolab challenges. They are a great way to share knowledge and explain the techniques used to solve a lab.

However, **public writeups are strictly forbidden while an event is still running**.

### During an event

You must not publicly publish or share:

- Complete or partial writeups
- Challenge solutions
- Flags or credentials
- Step-by-step instructions that reveal how to complete a challenge

::: danger Active events
Publishing a public writeup for a challenge while its event is still active may result in a **permanent ban from the Kenolab platform**.
:::

## After an event

Once the event has officially ended, you are free to publish your writeups.

In fact, **we encourage it!**

Writeups help the community learn new techniques, discover different approaches, and improve their cybersecurity skills.

Feel free to publish them on your personal blog, GitHub, or any other platform.

Thank you for helping us keep Kenolab fair, challenging, and enjoyable for everyone.
:::

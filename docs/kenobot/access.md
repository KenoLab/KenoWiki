# /access

Check your account and your seat, then get access to the event.

```text
/access
```

Run it after logging in on [www.kenolab.eu](https://www.kenolab.eu/login) with Discord and taking your seat in an event. Kenobot then gives you the event role, which unlocks the event channels and commands.

![Kenobot reply to the /access command](/images/how-to-play/access.png)

::: tip
On an event's own Discord server, `/access` can register you directly, without the website.
:::
